# Eitan — design context

## Design Context

### Users
Two adults, Assaf (אסף) and Natalie (נטלי), sharing one household. They use Eitan on their iPhones as an installed home-screen PWA: in quick moments between other things (in the car park, at the kitchen counter, late at night in bed). Their job is to get something off their mind ("remind us tomorrow at 16:00 to take Eitan to the doctor") and trust that it will come back as a push notification 30 minutes before. The chat with Eitan, the Claude-powered assistant, is the app. The task list, settings and family screens are secondary: you visit them to check, tick off or fix something.

Hebrew UI, RTL, Rubik. Light and dark follow the phone's setting and matter equally.

### Brand Personality
**Calm, warm, trustworthy.** It should feel like a shared note on the fridge between two people, not a productivity tool or a dashboard. Unhurried, quiet and domestic. Emotional goal: relief ("it's handled") rather than urgency. Copy is short, plain Hebrew, and addresses the couple in the plural.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Theme:** both light and dark, polished equally. Light is a warm paper off-white, not a cold blue-white. Dark is a warm charcoal that matches the app icon's charcoal tile, not a navy.
- **Color:** keep the sky-blue identity but refine it: a calmer, slightly deeper blue used rarely (primary action, my own chat bubbles, the active state). Neutrals carry a faint warm tint. Coral only for urgent or overdue items, sage for done. Color is never the only signal.
- **References:** Apple Reminders/Notes (inset grouped lists, quiet section headers, large titles); WhatsApp (a familiar chat: bubbles with a "tail" corner, a pill composer); Things 3 (calm, precise, spacious lists, round checkboxes).
- **Anti-references:** a SaaS dashboard; floating white cards with drop shadows everywhere; gradients; a "hero metric"; glassmorphism; anything loud or gamified.
- **Type:** Rubik for all text, including the large page titles and the login wordmark (full Hebrew coverage, and it's the face of the app-icon glyph). One typeface everywhere. Minimum text size is 12px (`--text-xs`). Form fields are 16px or larger (no iOS focus zoom).

### Design Principles
1. **The chat is home; everything else is quiet.** Secondary screens use the same calm grouped-list language and never compete with the chat.
2. **Fewer boxes.** One grouped container per section (Reminders style) instead of a shadowed card per row. Use hairlines and spacing to separate items, and save shadows for things that float (sheets, dialogs, toasts).
3. **No clutter in the chat.** Eitan's one-time question buttons stay slim and subtle (28px outline chips, light card) and vanish after a tap. No extra buttons, voice or WhatsApp.
4. **Accent is rare.** Blue marks the one thing to do or the current state. Coral means "needs attention now", and nothing else.
5. **Touch-first and honest.** Use 44px targets for primary controls. Motion is short and ease-out, uses only opacity and transform, and respects reduced motion.
